Welcome to Vellore Travels

Welcome to the Fort City of Tamil Nadu. Vellore has a rich blend of heritage and culture representing ancient Dravidian civilisation. From the historic seat of Pallavas, Cholas, and Nayaks, to its transition into a modern hub of education, research, and world-class medical facilities, Vellore invites you to explore its historic monuments and scenic spots.

FEATURED DESTINATION

Sripuram Golden Temple

Discover Sripuram, the spiritual marvel of Vellore. Built across 100 acres, this temple is covered in over 1.5 tonnes of pure gold foil, making it the largest golden temple in the world. Set along a star-shaped 1.8km pathway of spiritual teachings, Sripuram offers a colorful and dazzling view, sparkling brilliantly day and night.
