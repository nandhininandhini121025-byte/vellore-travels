Welcome To Vellore Travels

Welcome to the Fort City of Tamil Nadu. Vellore is a historic city that blends rich cultural heritage with modern developments, representing the ancient Dravidian civilisation. Known as the "Fort City," it is famous for the grand Vellore Fort built in the 16th century, the breathtaking Golden Temple (Sripuram) covered in pure gold, and top-tier educational and medical institutions like CMC and VIT. The city has been ruled by various dynasties including the Pallavas, Cholas, Vijayanagara Empire, Rashtrakutas, Carnatic kingdom, and the British, making it a prominent hub of history and tourism in South India.

Beyond its historical monuments, Vellore is also celebrated for its vibrant local culture, traditional cuisine, and scenic landscapes, including the nearby Yelagiri Hills and Jawadhu Hills. The region plays a crucial role in India's leather export industry and continues to grow as an advanced medical tourism destination, drawing visitors and patients from all over the world. Whether you are seeking spiritual peace, historical exploration, or top-notch healthcare, Vellore has something extraordinary to offer.
