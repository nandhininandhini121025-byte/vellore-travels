Welcome to Vellore Travels

Welcome to the Fort City of Tamil Nadu. Vellore has a rich blend of heritage and culture representing ancient Dravidian civilisation. From the historic seat of Pallavas, Cholas, and Nayaks, to its transition into a modern hub of education, research, and world-class medical facilities, Vellore invites you to explore its historic monuments and scenic spots.

Temples & Tourist Spots

Vellore offers a rich blend of history, spirituality, and natural beauty. From the majestic 16th-century Vellore Fort and the glittering Sripuram Golden Temple, to the serene landscapes of Amirthi Forest and Jalagamparai Waterfalls, there is something for every traveler. Discover the unique heritage, ancient temples, and vibrant culture that make this city a must-visit destination.
