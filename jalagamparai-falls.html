Tourst Places » Jalagamparai Water Falls

Jalagamparai water falls is located in the Yelagiri hills of the Vellore district of Tamil Nadu. Flowing down from a breathtaking height of 15 meters, the place is popular among lovebirds. Created by the Attaru river, which flows through the Yelagiri hills.

Jalagamparai waterfalls is a beautiful spot for picnic. The falls can be reached after a 1 hour trek of 6 km from Nilavoor. Best time to visit the falls is after the monsoon rains.
